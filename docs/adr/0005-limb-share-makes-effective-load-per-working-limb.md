# A limb share makes effective load a per-working-limb figure

Published bodyweight-contribution figures don't share a basis: a floor push-up's 0.64 is through
both hands, a split squat's 0.75 is on the front leg, so on one axis a squat and a pistol both read
1.0. Rather than halve every bilateral seed to a per-limb figure (floor push-up 0.32, untraceable
to its source), a node keeps its published contribution and carries a second seeded number, the
**limb share**: the fraction of that load on the working limb, 0.5 for bilateral, 1.0 for one-limb,
in between for staggered stances (b-stance ≈ 0.7). Effective load is then defined per working
limb, (bodyweight × contribution + external load) × share, and orders squat → b-stance → split →
Bulgarian → airborne as it should. The share also replaces a unilateral flag: a node with a share
above 0.5 has a working side and its performances log reps per side.
