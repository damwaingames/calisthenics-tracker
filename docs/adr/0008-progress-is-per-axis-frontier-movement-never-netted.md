# Progress is the frontier moving on any one axis, never netted across axes

Each axis of overload runs its own independent double progression and each counts as progress on
its own: more volume at a node, a harder parameter, more external load, a new furthest node on the
chain. A step up the chain is progress even though reps drop; nothing is ever flagged as a loss,
so a deload or a layoff needs no special marking. Bodyweight change is a readout, not progress or
regression: it shows up indirectly as the chain moving faster or slower. Effective load is the
readout that lets nodes be compared, never the judge. We rejected scoring a performance on
effective load × volume, or on any weighted blend, because every weighting is wrong for
someone's week, and rejected "the app never judges at all" because "you've done something you
hadn't before" is a verdict it can give honestly.
